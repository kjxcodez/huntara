Philosophy

Transform. Execute. Grow.

Everything in HUNTARA follows one loop. Raw information becomes structured intelligence, structured intelligence becomes executed outreach, and executed outreach becomes pipeline — without ever leaving your machine.

01 — Transform

Turn a search into structured companies and contacts

Discovery runs entirely on your desktop hardware.

  • Maps discovery — headless search finds companies by industry and geography.
  • Website crawling — a breadth-first crawler pulls verified emails and phone numbers from each domain.
  • Decision-maker enrichment — finds the CEO, founder, or VP behind each company, not just a generic inbox.
02 — Execute

Run the outreach without leaving the app

Sequences, sending, and replies stay under one roof.

  • Sequence builder — drag-and-drop steps for waits, conditions, and sends, run in a sandboxed worker.
  • Direct SMTP sending — your own mail credentials, rate-limited and check-pointed, no shared sending pool.
  • Reply detection — an inbox poller stops a sequence the moment a contact replies, bounces, or unsubscribes.
03 — Grow

Know which leads are worth your next hour

Every company is scored, not just stored.

  • Opportunity scoring — fit, size, intent, and urgency combine into one 0–100 score, sorted into Hot, Warm, and Cold queues.
  • Intent signals — pricing pages, trial sign-ups, and case studies are read directly off a company's own site.
  • AI-drafted opening lines — grounded in the pain points found during discovery, not generic templates.
Architecture

Local-first, not local-only

Every read and write hits a workspace-isolated SQLite database on your machine first. A sync engine pushes changes to the cloud in the background, treating the network as a transport layer — not a dependency.

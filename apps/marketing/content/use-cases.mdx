# Target Use Cases

HUNTARA supports local outbound prospecting workflows, making it ideal for growth teams, founders, and agencies.

## Local B2B Lead Generation

Targeting local businesses requires compiling directories of verified companies and managers.

- **Objective**: Identify local service agencies, clinics, or contractors in target areas.
- **Workflow**: Run regional directory crawlers across cities, harvest contact details from business sites, and export the resulting lists directly to your local CRM.
- **Value**: Acquire accurate local lists without paying per-lead premium database fees.

## Scalable Outreach Personalization

Generic outreach campaigns suffer from low conversion rates. Personalizing emails at scale is often bottlenecked by manual research time.

- **Objective**: Personalize outreach templates based on target website contents and executive titles.
- **Workflow**: Crawl company websites to extract target details, run local AI agents to analyze company positioning, and write tailored email opening lines.
- **Value**: Improve response rates by sending emails tailored to each prospect's actual business focus.

## Private Offline Campaign Audits

Many organizations want to draft, preview, and test outreach sequences before deploying them online.

- **Objective**: Design campaigns, compile lead lists, and test email rendering offline.
- **Workflow**: Enroll contacts in draft sequences, evaluate scoring models locally, and preview personalized draft emails before sending.
- **Value**: Iterate on outbound campaigns without exposing sensitive SMTP configurations or client lists to third-party cloud stores.

# Core Platform Features

HUNTARA provides a suite of local prospecting engines to automate business discovery, lead verification, and campaign delivery.

## Business Discovery

Search and extract listings using built-in maps and web crawlers:
- **Directory Search**: Automatically search target regions and parse business listing elements.
- **Redirect Resolution**: Resolve website redirects to find canonical root domains.
- **Listing Data Extraction**: Harvest business names, addresses, phone numbers, ratings, and social URLs.

## Deep Web Crawling

Analyze discovered websites using depth-controlled crawlers:
- **Contact Harvesting**: Scan public pages to extract emails, phone numbers, and social media handles.
- **Spam Filtering**: Automatically ignore tracking pixels, tracking links, and false contact scripts.
- **Compliance Rules**: Obeys standard exclusion guidelines to scrape targets responsibly.

## Contact Enrichment & Verification

Enrich prospect lists with verified executive contact details:
- **Decision Maker Identification**: Match prospects with company personnel based on public web records and social directories.
- **Email Quality Validation**: Validate MX records, syntax, and disposable domain filters before scheduling outreach.

## Outreach Sequences

Draft and coordinate personalized outreach sequences:
- **Dynamic Variable Injection**: Inject personalized fields (e.g., recipient name, business type, local city) into email templates.
- **SMTP Send Scheduler**: Limit send rates and enforce domain pacing to protect IP and mailbox reputation.
- **IMAP Reply Tracking**: Listen to incoming replies, correlate them with sent outreach, and automatically pause sequences for replied contacts.
